## 1. What product are we planning to build?
An app which helps students in task management, revision and progeress tracking. they can create to-do lists, set deadlines, and update task statuses. They can create flashcards while studying and us ethem for revision. They can see how much owrk they have completed and what is still pending. 

## 2. What problem does it solve?
It helps students who struggle to keep track of deadlines, assignmemnts and pending topics. It helps students manage their study materials. It also help them remeber which topic needs revision and also makes it easy for them to track their study progress. 

## 3. Who are the target users?
Any student in general who finds it hard to organise their study tasks and keep track of their revision. 

## 4. What is the main use case?
A student opens 'StudyLoop', add their subjects, create study tasks with deadlines, create flashcards for important topics, marks tasks as its completed and check thier progress. 

## 5. What are the core features?
The core features are Dashboards, Subject management, Flashcard creation and revision, Study planner and task tracking, progress overview with light and dark themes. 

## 6. What assumptions are we making about users?
We assume that students want their activities to be organised in one place and prefer simple interfaces over complicated study applications. They would also want to create their own flashcards. Keeping track of completed tasks might encourage students to stay consistent.  

## 7. What will be included in our MVP?
- Adding and managing subjects
- Creating and revising flashcards
- Adding and completing study tasks
- Viewing study progress 
- Saving data locally

## 8. What will NOT be included in our MVP?
Payment or subscription features & collaboration between students.
