# StudyLoop Wireframe Notes
Use these notes to draw your own wireframe in Figma, Excalidraw or on paper and export it as an image/PDF.

## Shared shell
- Left navigation: brand, Dashboard, My Subjects, Flashcards, Study Planner, Progress.
- Top bar: current page breadcrumb and student profile.

## Dashboard
- Greeting and short introduction.
- Main call-to-action: Add a study task.
- Weekly momentum banner.
- Four statistic cards.
- Two columns: subject list and today's task checklist.

## My Subjects
- Page title and explanation.
- Add-subject form.
- Grid of subject cards with code, flashcard count and revision progress.

## Flashcards
- Page title and create-flashcard button.
- Subject label and card count.
- Large question/answer card; click to reveal answer.
- Previous and next controls.

## Study Planner
- Add-task form with subject selector.
- List of tasks with completion checkboxes and status.

## Progress
- Completion percentage summary.
- Summary cards for subjects, flashcards, completed tasks and remaining tasks.

## User flow
Dashboard → My Subjects (add a subject) → Flashcards (create/revise a card) → Study Planner (add/complete a task) → Progress (view completion summary).
